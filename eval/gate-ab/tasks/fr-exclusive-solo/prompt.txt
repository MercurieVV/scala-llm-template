Add exclusive solo to the mixer. MixerMode.exclusive(strips, perBank) builds a MixerMode with exclusive solo; new MixerMode(strips, perBank) keeps today's behaviour. In an exclusive mixer, soloing an instrument clears every other solo, and the solo lamps of visible strips whose solo was cleared go dark. Pressing the solo of the only soloed instrument un-solos it, as today. Stop when you are done.
