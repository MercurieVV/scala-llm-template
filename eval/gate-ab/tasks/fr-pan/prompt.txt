Add panning to the mixer. Turning a strip's V-Pot (Control.Encoder) in MixerMode pans that strip's instrument: each detent moves the pan by 0.05, pan starts at 0.0 (centre) and is clamped to -1.0 .. 1.0. MixerMode emits the new command Command.SetPan(instrument: String, pan: Double) only when the pan actually changed, and turning a V-Pot never changes a level. Everything else keeps working as it does today. Stop when you are done.
