Let a song choose its tuning reference. Notes takes the A4 reference pitch in Hz as a constructor parameter: final class Notes[V](a4: Double)(using ConvertableTo[V], Field[V]), so Notes[Double](432.0) tunes everything to A4 = 432 Hz and Notes[Double](440.0) is today's tuning. Every pitch Notes gives — pitch, justPitch and the note names — scales with the reference. Existing songs keep sounding exactly as they do now. Stop when you are done.
